# Founding creators: plan (parked)

Status: planned, not built. Mockups were made on the real app (founding creator badge on cards and
profiles, "Bring 3 friends" card, personal invite link sheet, Admin → Creators).

## 1. The goal

Get 50 real people who post regularly, not 50 badges. One person posting 3 good polls a week (and
sharing them in their group chats) is worth more than 500 sign-ups.

## 2. Who to pick

Look for people who already start conversations, not people with big follower counts:

- **The group-chat starter:** the friend who always posts "Guys, settle this: …" on WhatsApp.
- **College reps and club heads** (fests, debate and quiz clubs, placement cells): they reach hundreds
  of 18–22 year olds.
- **Niche voices:** a cricket fan, a Bollywood buff, a finance/IPO nerd, a foodie, an office-memes
  person, a tech-Twitter person. One per topic keeps the feed varied.
- **Small creators** on Instagram or X with 1k–20k followers who post opinions: big enough to bring
  people, small enough to care.
- **Mix of cities:** Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, plus a few tier-2 cities.

Avoid friends who'll join to be nice and never post. One active creator beats five polite ones.

## 3. Roll it out in waves

| Wave | Who | Size | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (week 1) | Close friends who already debate a lot | 10 | Prove the loop: they post, their friends vote and join |
| 2 (weeks 2–3) | College reps, niche voices | 20 | Variety and new circles |
| 3 (week 4+) | Small creators, plus the "bring 3 friends" path | 20 | Reach beyond your network |

Show the scarcity ("12 of 50 taken") from day one. It's real, and it creates urgency.

## 4. How to ask (copy-paste)

**WhatsApp, to a friend:**

> Hey! I'm building Eithr, a poll app where people settle debates (cricket, Bollywood, office
> drama, food fights). I'm picking 50 founding creators and you're one of the first people I
> thought of, because you're always the one starting debates 😄
> You get a permanent ✦ Founding creator badge, your number (#7), creator stats, and you'd shape
> what we build next. Your link (works once): <link>

**DM, to a small creator:**

> Love your takes on <topic>. I'm building Eithr, a poll app for India where every question is a
> debate, and I'm hand-picking 50 founding creators. No posting schedule or contract: post polls on
> <topic> when you feel like it, and your followers vote and argue. You'd get a ✦ badge, your
> founding number, and stats on every poll. Interested? <link>

Tips: personal messages, never a broadcast. Say why them. Never promise money yet.

## 5. Their first day (in the app)

1. The invite link opens "You're invited to be a founding creator #7" → Continue with Google.
2. Welcome: "You're founding creator #7 ✦", then three idea starters for their topic (no blank
   page): "Post your first poll in 30 seconds."
3. After the first poll: "Share it to your group chat." The first votes come from their own friends,
   which is how new users join.
4. Their first vote milestones (3, 5, 10) arrive as notifications (already built). That early
   dopamine is what brings them back.

## 6. What keeps them posting

Status and attention matter more than features:

- **A private WhatsApp group** of founding creators, with you in it: share wins ("this poll got 400
  votes"), feedback, ideas.
- **Weekly spotlight:** "Creator of the week", pinned for a day, plus a shout-out on Eithr's
  Instagram or X.
- **Creator stats** (views, votes, best poll, vote rate): people post more when they can see their
  impact.
- **A weekly prompt** in the group ("This week: IPL auction / Diwali / office life"), fed by the
  Cowork trend list.
- **First access** to new features, and their names on a "Founding creators" page.
- **Later, if it works:** creator rewards (merch, cash for the top poll of the month). Not now.

Light expectations, stated kindly: "Try to post 2–3 polls a week." No penalty; creators who go
quiet for 30 days can lose the "active" glow on their badge, but the badge itself stays forever.

## 7. Rules

- The badge is permanent and numbered (that's what makes it special). Removed only for abuse:
  spam, hate, or fake votes.
- No buying or selling invites. Each link works once and is tied to who you sent it to.
- Founding creators follow the same community guidelines; their polls go through the same reporting.

## 8. Numbers to watch (could live in Admin → Metrics)

- **Activation:** % who post within 48 hours (aim for 70%+).
- **Weekly active creators:** posted at least 1 poll this week (aim for 60%+).
- **Polls per creator per week,** and **votes per poll.**
- **New users per creator:** people who joined through their polls or links. The one that matters.

## 9. What to build

**Must have:**

- ✦ badge with number on feed cards, profiles, comments and leaderboards.
- One-time invite links (Admin → Creators: make a link, see who used it).
- Welcome screen with topic starters.
- Creator stats card on their profile.
- 10 polls a day for founding creators (others stay at 5).

**Nice to have (later):** "Bring 3 friends" path (hold until wave 3, so the first 30 are
hand-picked), "active" glow, Creator of the week, a Founding creators page, the metrics above.
